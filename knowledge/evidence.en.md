# Public evidence

Evidence should distinguish between published work, locally demonstrated work and work still evolving.

- Published case studies and materials are available on the [personal website](https://ismailnasry.it/).
- [Mate Smart Lock](https://ismailnasry.it/project/mate-smart-lock/) is published on Snap Store and GitHub.
- [AI Media SEO Metadata](https://ismailnasry.it/project/ai-media-seo-metadata/) is a published case study for a plugin in evolution.
- The Ideas Workshop demonstrates a navigable 3D experience, avatar mode, interactive activities and an accessible fallback.

Metrics should always be read in the context of their source case study rather than presented as universal benchmarks.

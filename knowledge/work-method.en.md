# Work method

1. **Understand** - clarify the problem, people, context and constraints.
2. **Shape** - organize content, data, interactions and responsibilities.
3. **Build** - choose the right stack and create a verifiable prototype.
4. **Test** - check usability, accessibility, performance and responsive behavior.
5. **Release** - document decisions, limits and what can evolve.

A product is not finished when it is published: it should remain understandable and maintainable.

# Experience and contribution

This page describes responsibilities demonstrated across public projects without inventing employment titles or undocumented timelines.

- Defining the question, context and constraints.
- Modeling architecture, roles, permissions and interactions.
- Building prototypes and features.
- Connecting content, data and AI tools.
- Testing usability, accessibility, performance and coherence.
- Documenting, publishing and iterating.

Examples include SkillUp domain modeling and learning flows, AI Media SEO Metadata batch workflows, Mate Smart Lock’s Linux application and the modular 3D content system behind The Ideas Workshop.

# FAQ

## What does Ideas Developer mean?

It describes hybrid work: turning ideas into systems, products and experiences by connecting development, AI, 3D and narrative.

## Are you a developer, designer or creative?

The profile crosses all three areas, but the starting point is the product: understanding what needs to become possible and building a clear way to make it real.

## Which project best represents your approach?

The Ideas Workshop, because it combines editorial structure, web development, 3D, interaction, accessibility and Markdown-driven updates.
